1. Information We Collect

When you use Prepwire, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, information generated through your use of the platform, and certain technical information required for the website to operate.

Information you provide

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if you choose to provide one
  • Profile picture, if you choose to upload one
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Other information you voluntarily provide to us

Automatically generated information

We may also collect information related to how you use Prepwire, such as practice activity, selected subjects, examination type, scores, progress, streaks, and other information necessary to provide the platform's features.

2. Account Information

If you create an account on Prepwire, we collect information needed to create and maintain your account.

Your email address may be used for authentication, account verification, password recovery, security notifications, and other account-related communications.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and should notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

3. Usage and Activity Information

Prepwire is designed to help users prepare for examinations through practice and progress tracking. To provide these features, we may store information about your activity on the platform.

  • Questions attempted
  • Questions answered correctly or incorrectly
  • Practice session results
  • Scores and percentages
  • Subjects practiced
  • Examination types selected
  • Practice dates and times
  • Progress statistics
  • Streak information
  • Practice history

This information is primarily used to provide personalized progress tracking and improve your learning experience.

4. Practice and Academic Data

Information generated from your practice sessions may be associated with your account so that Prepwire can display your performance and learning progress.

Your practice results are intended to help you understand your performance. Prepwire does not represent practice scores as official examination results.

We may use aggregated or non-identifying statistics to understand how users interact with our educational features and to improve Prepwire.

5. Profile Images

If you choose to upload a profile image, the image may be stored by our storage provider and associated with your account.

Uploading a profile image is optional. You may remove or replace your profile image through the available account settings, where those features are provided.

Please avoid uploading images containing sensitive personal information or information belonging to another person without permission.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Prepwire may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage, and session storage to provide and remember certain settings and features.

These technologies may be used to store information such as:

  • Theme preferences
  • Practice settings
  • Temporary practice-session information
  • Authentication-related information
  • User interface preferences

Some browser-stored information may remain on your device until you clear your browser's storage or until the information is removed by the website.

7. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users
  • Verify email addresses
  • Provide CBT practice features
  • Save practice history and results
  • Display progress and performance statistics
  • Maintain streaks and other learning features
  • Save your preferences and settings
  • Provide account recovery features
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of Prepwire
  • Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
  • Communicate important service-related information
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to operating, maintaining, securing, and improving Prepwire unless otherwise permitted or required by law.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share or provide access to information with service providers that help us operate Prepwire. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide the services they support.

We may also disclose information when:

  • You give us permission to do so.
  • It is necessary to provide a service you requested.
  • It is required by applicable law or legal process.
  • It is necessary to protect the security of Prepwire or its users.
  • It is necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity.

9. Third-Party Services

Prepwire may use third-party service providers to operate certain parts of the platform.

Supabase

We use Supabase for services such as authentication, database functionality, and potentially file storage. Information submitted through Prepwire may therefore be processed by Supabase as part of providing these services.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms governing how they process information.

Other service providers

As Prepwire develops, we may use additional providers for hosting, analytics, email delivery, storage, security, or other infrastructure. Where appropriate, this policy may be updated to reflect material changes to the services we use.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use a strong, unique password for your Prepwire account and avoid sharing your login credentials with others.

If you believe that your account or personal information has been compromised, please contact us as soon as possible.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain your account, provide requested features, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal, technical, and operational requirements.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to certain processing of your information.
  • Request information about how your data is processed.

Some requests may be subject to applicable legal or operational limitations.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below.

13. Children's Privacy

Prepwire is an educational platform intended to support students preparing for examinations.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information in violation of applicable requirements, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Prepwire develops, our services change, or applicable laws and requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to periodically review this page to stay informed about how we handle information.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way Prepwire handles your information, please contact us.
